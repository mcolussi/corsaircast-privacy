# Corsair Cast — Privacy Policy

*Last updated: 15 October 2026*

Corsair Cast is a podcast player made by one independent developer. It has no accounts, no analytics, no ads
and no server of its own. **The developer does not collect, see or sell any data about you.**

## What stays on your device

Your subscriptions, playlists, listening history, playback positions, settings, downloads and transcripts are
stored on your iPhone. Transcripts are made on the device by Apple's speech recognition.

## iCloud sync

If you're signed in to iCloud, your library syncs between your devices through **your own private iCloud
account** (Apple's CloudKit). The developer cannot read it. Apple's handling of that data is covered by
[Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/). Reset App in Settings erases it from iCloud too.

## Who the app talks to

- **Podcast publishers and their hosts**, to fetch feeds, artwork, episodes and show notes — the same requests
  any podcast app makes. Those servers see your IP address, as with any download.
- **Apple's iTunes Search**, when you search for a podcast or the app looks up missing artwork.
- **Apple**, for Siri requests, notifications and in-app purchases. Purchases are handled entirely by the App
  Store; the app never sees your payment details.

## Created chapters (Premium)

When you ask Corsair Cast to create chapters for an episode, it sends that episode's transcript and a list of
topics from its show notes to **Anthropic's Claude**, which returns the chapters. Nothing else is sent — no name,
account, contact details or listening history — and the request isn't linked to you. Anthropic doesn't use this
data to train its models and deletes it after a short retention period; see
[Anthropic's Privacy Policy](https://www.anthropic.com/legal/privacy). Nothing is sent unless you have Premium and
choose to create chapters.

## Send Feedback

Send Feedback opens an email draft in your own mail app. Nothing is sent unless you send it. If you choose to
attach the diagnostic log, it is redacted first and goes only to the developer, who uses it only to fix problems.

## Children

The app does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

## Changes

If a future version sends any data off your device that isn't described here, this page will be updated
before that version is released.
